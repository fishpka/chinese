十鼓文创园区

文化詞彙文化詞彙

解說

十鼓文创园区座落于台南市的郊区，透过静谧的森林小径与繁华的市区相隔，地理位置邻近台南市区，为民众提供了一个亲近文化艺术的新据点。这片园区昔日是台湾糖业公司仁德车路墘糖厂，经历多年闲置后，焕发出百年历史的糖厂风采，融入了十鼓独特的台湾特色鼓乐，成为亚洲第一座以鼓乐主题的国际艺术村。 十鼓文创园区与奇美博物馆、保安车站、虎山农场、台南都会公园相连，形成了仁德文化园区（86艺术聚落），同时也成为南台湾的艺文新地标。除了十鼓击乐团赢得葛莱美奖、美国独立音乐大奖和臺湾金曲奖的肯定外，园区的经营和多项建筑建设也多次获得建筑园冶奖和世界建筑节大奖的高度评价。

英文翻譯

The Ten Drum Cultural and Creative Park is located in the suburbs of Tainan City, separated from the bustling city by tranquil forest paths. Its geographical proximity to the Tainan city center provides the public with a new focal point for embracing culture and the arts. This area was formerly the Tairyo Sugar Corporation's Rende Cheloqian Sugar Refinery, which, after years of abandonment, has been revitalized to showcase the century-old history of the sugar refinery. It has seamlessly integrated the unique Taiwanese drum music of the Ten Drum Art Percussion Group, establishing itself as the first international art village with a drum music theme in Asia. The Ten Drum Cultural and Creative Park is connected to the Chimei Museum, Bao'an Railway Station, Husan Farm, and Tainan Metropolitan Park, forming the Rende Cultural Park (86 Artistic Settlement). Simultaneously, it has become a new cultural landmark in southern Taiwan. In addition to the Ten Drum Percussion Group's recognition with Grammy Awards, Independent Music Awards, and Taiwan Golden Melody Awards, the park's management and various architectural projects have also received acclaim from the Architecture Landscape Awards and the World Architecture Festival Awards.
