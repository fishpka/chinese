鞭炮

文化詞彙 · 喜悅

中文解釋

鞭炮的由来可追溯到古代中国。相传，鞭炮最早是用来驱赶恶灵和邪鬼的。传说在古代，有一种叫"Nian"的凶恶怪兽，每年都在春节时出现，吞食村庄的人畜。村民们发现，Nian最害怕火光和巨响，于是开始用敲锣、打鼓、点燃火把和燃放鞭炮来驱赶Nian。 随着时间的推移，这种驱邪的习俗逐渐演变成了春节庆祝的一部分，而鞭炮也成为了欢庆和驱邪的象征。现在，在中国的传统节日和庆典上，人们仍然习惯性地燃放鞭炮，寓意驱逐邪恶、迎接好运。

English

The origin of firecrackers can be traced back to ancient China. According to legend, firecrackers were originally used to drive away evil spirits and demons. In ancient times, there was a ferocious monster called "Nian" that appeared every year during the Spring Festival, devouring both humans and livestock in villages. The villagers discovered that Nian was most afraid of fire and loud noises, so they began using drums, gongs, lit torches, and set off firecrackers to drive away Nian. As time passed, this tradition of warding off evil gradually evolved into a part of the Spring Festival celebration, and firecrackers became a symbol of joy and warding off evil. Today, during traditional Chinese festivals and celebrations, people still habitually set off firecrackers, symbolizing the expulsion of negativity and welcoming good fortune.
