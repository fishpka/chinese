團圓

文化詞彙

中文解釋

昨天是冬至，你吃湯圓了嗎？冬至是傳統的節氣之一，通常發生在每年的12月21日或22日。這一天標誌著北半球的冬季開始，同時也是陽光最短、黑夜最長的一天。 冬至有著悠久的歷史和濃厚的文化底蘊。人們認為冬至是陰氣達到極致的時候，因此這天被視為陰陽交替的分界點，也是陽氣漸長的起點。 在冬至這天，人們有著一系列的傳統習俗和活動。其中一項重要的習俗是吃湯圓，因為湯圓在中文中的發音與「團圓」相近，象徵著家庭的團聚和圓滿。

English

Yesterday was the Winter Solstice. Did you have tangyuan (glutinous rice dumplings)? The Winter Solstice is one of the traditional Chinese solar terms, typically occurring on December 21st or 22nd each year. This day marks the beginning of winter in the Northern Hemisphere, as well as the shortest daylight and longest night of the year. The Winter Solstice has a rich history and profound cultural significance. It is believed to be the peak of yin energy, signifying the transition between yin and yang, and the starting point of the gradual increase of yang energy. On the Winter Solstice, people engage in a series of traditional customs and activities. One significant tradition is eating tangyuan, as the pronunciation of "tangyuan" in Chinese is similar to "tuanyuan," symbolizing family reunion and completeness.

Français

Hier était le solstice d'hiver. As-tu mangé des tangyuan (boulettes de riz glutineux) ? Le solstice d'hiver est l'un des termes solaires traditionnels chinois, se produisant généralement le 21 ou 22 décembre chaque année. Ce jour marque le début de l'hiver dans l'hémisphère nord, ainsi que le jour le plus court et la nuit la plus longue de l'année.
