将进酒

典故 · 喜悅

中文解釋

「将进酒」是唐代诗人李白的诗，藉由这首诗表达他的人生观：「人生苦短，应当及时行乐，不虚度光阴。」 其中的名句如：君不见黄河之水天上来，奔流到海不復回。 这些句子展现了他对自由、生命与豪情的热爱，也隐含他对理想不得志的抒发。

English

"Bring in the Wine" (Jiāng Jìn Jiǔ) is a poem by the Tang Dynasty poet Li Bai. In this poem, he expresses his view on life: “Life is short — one should enjoy it in time and not let it pass in vain.” One of its famous lines is: "Have you not seen the waters of the Yellow River coming down from the sky, rushing to the sea and never returning?" These verses reflect his love of freedom, life, and unrestrained passion, while also subtly expressing his frustration at being unable to realize his ideals.
