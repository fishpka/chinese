月老

文化詞彙

中文解釋

龙山寺，一座充满历史和文化底蕴的宝地，见证了台湾数百年的变迁与发展。这次拍摄，我们将以「月老」这位掌管姻缘的神祇为主题，探讨爱情、缘分与人际连结的深意。 首先，影片会带观众走进龙山寺，感受这座庙宇的庄严与神圣。随后，镜头聚焦在月老神像上，细腻展示凋刻工艺和红线细节，传递出古老传说的浪漫色彩。我们将採访几位前来祈求姻缘的信众，聆听他们的心声和祈愿，让观众能够感受到真实而动人的情感。 在音乐和光影的配合下，影片将营造出一种神秘而温暖的氛围，象徵着月老不仅仅是信仰中的守护者，更是爱与希望的象徵。最后，我们将展示信众们在祈愿后，获得美满姻缘的幸福场景，呈现出月老带来的美好祝福。

English

"Longshan Temple, a treasure trove rich in history and cultural heritage, has witnessed the transformation and development of Taiwan over centuries. In this filming, we will focus on 'Yue Lao,' the deity who governs marriage and relationships, to explore the profound meanings of love, fate, and human connections. First, the film will take viewers inside Longshan Temple, allowing them to experience the solemnity and sanctity of this temple. The camera will then focus on the statue of Yue Lao, delicately showcasing the intricate carving craftsmanship and the details of the red strings, conveying the romantic essence of ancient legends. We will interview several worshippers who come to pray for marriage, listening to their heartfelt wishes and desires, enabling the audience to feel the genuine and touching emotions. With the interplay of music and light, the film will create a mysterious yet warm atmosphere, symbolizing that Yue Lao is not just a guardian in faith but also a symbol of love and hope. Finally, we will depict scenes of worshippers finding happiness in marriage after making their wishes, showcasing the beautiful blessings brought by Yue Lao."
