點石成金

成語

中文解釋

"點石成金"意思是通过一种神奇的力量或手段，使得普通的事物或情况转变为非常宝贵或有价值的东西。通常用来形容某人非凡的能力或智慧可以让平凡的事物变得非常有用或有影响力。

English

"Turning stone into gold" means using a magical power or method to transform ordinary things or situations into something extremely valuable or precious. It's typically used to describe someone's extraordinary ability or wisdom to make ordinary things very useful or influential.
