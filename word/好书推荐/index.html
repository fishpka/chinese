Book Recommendation: The Adventurous Course of Being 16 While traveling, one might occasionally encounter young backpackers who set out on foreign journeys at the age of 18 or 19. Away from home, they rely entirely on themselves, handling everything from booking flights and obtaining visas to managing food, clothing, shelter, transportation, education, and leisure during their travels, as well as mundane tasks like fetching water and cooking. Besides being independent, they are articulate and eloquent, unafraid to express their thoughts regardless of their life experiences, and have accumulated a wealth of stories. Are you ready to embark on a journey? Before you set off, why not imagine the world through the perspectives of these twelve travelers? And after arriving, cast aside the various narratives described in the book, heighten your senses, and immerse yourself in experiencing vastly different cultures firsthand. 好书推荐：十六岁的壮游课 旅行路上偶尔会遇见年轻的背包客，十八、九岁就离家到异国旅行。出门在外所有事都靠自己，从预订机票办理签证、行程中的食衣住行育乐、生活上的打水煮饭⋯⋯样样都得亲力亲为。除了独立自主，他们各个口齿一伶俐、能言善辩，无论人生经验多寡，都勇于表达自己的想法，也累积了一身的好故事。 准备好要踏上旅程了吗？在出发之前，不妨用十二 位旅人的视角开启对世界的想像。而在抵达之后，请抛下书中叙述的种种，放大自己的五感，用肉身体验截然不同的文化。
