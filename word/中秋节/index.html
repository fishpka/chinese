中秋节

文化詞彙 · 喜悅

中文解釋

中秋节 中秋节的日期是每年的农曆8月15日，亲朋好友会一起团圆赏月吃月饼、吃柚子、烤肉，月饼的故事要从历史故事说起了！元朝时期，蒙古部族残暴统治，百姓苦不堪言，汉人起义，朱元璋为其中之一。当时朝廷监控严密，消息难以传递，朱元璋陷入困境。他的军师刘伯温提出巧妙计策：将消息藏于月饼内。这样，「八月十五夜起义」的讯息成功传达到各地起义军手中。在八月十五当天，各地义军联合攻下元朝，朱元璋成为明朝开国皇帝。

English

The Mid-Autumn Festival falls on the 15th day of the lunar calendar every year. During this time, family and friends gather together to enjoy the full moon, indulge in mooncakes, eat pomelos, and barbecue. The story of mooncakes has its roots in history! During the Yuan Dynasty, when the Mongol tribes ruled with cruelty and the people suffered greatly, Han Chinese rebelled, and Zhu Yuanzhang was one of them. At that time, communication was tightly monitored by the government, making it difficult for news to spread. Zhu Yuanzhang found himself in a dire situation. His strategist, Liu Bowen, devised a clever plan: hiding the message within mooncakes. This way, the message of the "uprising on the 15th night of the eighth month" successfully reached the rebel forces across the country. On the 15th day of the eighth lunar month, rebel forces from various regions united and overthrew the Yuan Dynasty, and Zhu Yuanzhang became the founding emperor of the Ming Dynasty.
