余音绕梁

典故

中文解釋

"余音绕梁"的意思是形容音乐或歌声优美动听，结束后好像声音还在回荡，给人留下深刻的印象。它常用来形容音乐或歌声的余韵悠长，令人回味无穷。

English

The meaning of "余音绕梁" (yú yīn rào liáng) is to describe music or singing that is so beautiful and melodious that even after it ends, the sound seems to linger in the air, leaving a deep impression. It is often used to describe the long-lasting resonance of music or singing, which makes people reminisce.
