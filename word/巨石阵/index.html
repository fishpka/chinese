巨石阵

典故

中文解釋

巨石阵位于英格兰威尔特郡埃姆斯伯里，由数十块高达六公尺的巨石组成，形成一个巨大的圆圈，据推测，这个圆形石林已在这片平坦的土地上屹立了数千年。1986年，巨石阵连同埃夫伯里和相关遗址一同被列为世界文化遗产。 然而目前为止人们仍无法确切得知这座建筑的原因。有科学家认为，圆形石林可能是早期英国部落或宗教组织举行仪式的中心。或是可能用于观察天文现象，人们可能在季节变化之际在此举办各种活动。儘管巨石阵的历史充满谜团，但它仍然是一个引人入胜的历史宝藏，吸引着无数探索者和文化爱好者。

English

The Stonehenge is located in Amesbury, Wiltshire, England, and is composed of dozens of towering stones, some reaching up to six meters in height, arranged in a massive circle. It is estimated that this circular stone formation has stood on the flat land for thousands of years. In 1986, Stonehenge, along with Avebury and associated sites, was designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. However, the exact purpose of this structure remains elusive to this day. Some scientists believe that the circular stone arrangement may have served as a central location for rituals conducted by early British tribes or religious organizations. Alternatively, it could have been used for observing astronomical phenomena, with people possibly organizing various activities during seasonal changes. Despite the mysteries surrounding the history of Stonehenge, it continues to be a captivating historical treasure, attracting numerous explorers and cultural enthusiasts.
